Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

TwoUp collects nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no TwoUp server for anything to be sent to. Your workspaces, notes and imported documents stay on your device.

1. Who this covers

This policy describes how the TwoUp iOS app ("TwoUp", "the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the app as distributed on the Apple App Store. Because the app has no backend, most of this document explains what does not happen.

2. Information we collect

None. Measured against the categories Apple asks developers to declare:

We do not build a profile of you, and there is nothing for us to sell or share, because nothing reaches us in the first place.

3. What TwoUp stores on your device

The app keeps the following inside its own storage area on your iPhone, where only TwoUp can read it:

  • Your saved workspaces — which panes are paired and how they are arranged.
  • The text of your notes.
  • Copies of documents you import, plus the addresses of sites you save as shortcuts and the icons for them.
  • Reading and layout preferences.
  • Website data belonging to pages you open in the web pane — cookies and caches, exactly as a browser keeps them.

None of this is uploaded, and none of it is synced between your devices. Copies of imported documents are deliberately excluded from your iCloud backup: a large file you already keep in Files should not be duplicated into every backup you make.

4. Pages you open in the web pane

TwoUp's web pane uses WKWebView, the same web engine Safari uses. When you open a page, your device contacts that website directly, just as any browser would. That website — and anything it embeds — receives your IP address and may set cookies, and its own privacy policy governs what it does with that.

What we want to be exact about: your traffic does not pass through us, we do not inject any tracking into the pages you view, and your browsing history is not recorded off your device. Website data lives in the app's storage and is removed when you delete the app.

5. Site titles and icons

When you save a site as a shortcut, TwoUp requests that page over HTTPS to read its title and icon so the shortcut is recognisable. Only the address you asked for is contacted; the request uses a temporary session that keeps nothing on disk, and no third party is involved.

6. Purchases

TwoUp Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment — we never see your name, your Apple Account or your card. What the app receives is Apple's answer, on your device, to a single question: whether Pro is currently unlocked. Apple's handling of the transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

7. No analytics, advertising or tracking

TwoUp contains no analytics or attribution SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party code of any kind. It does not use the advertising identifier and never shows the App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is no tracking to ask permission for.

8. Permissions

The app requests no system permissions — no camera, photo library, microphone, contacts or location. Documents arrive through the system file picker, which grants access only to the file you personally choose.

9. Children's privacy

TwoUp is not directed at children under 13, but it collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. There is no data for a parent to request or for us to delete.

10. Retention, deletion and your rights

Everything the app holds lives on your device and is under your control. Delete an individual workspace, note or document inside the app; delete the app itself and all of it — including saved website data — is removed with it.

Rights under the GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and comparable laws — access, correction, deletion, portability, objection — apply to personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to export, correct or erase, and no data broker to opt out of. If you would like that confirmed in writing, ask us and we will confirm it.

11. Security

Your data sits inside the app's sandbox, protected by iOS and by your device passcode and encryption. Network requests the app makes are HTTPS only, enforced by App Transport Security. No system is perfectly secure, but keeping data off the internet removes most of what could go wrong with it.

12. Changes to this policy

If TwoUp ever gains a feature that touches your data — syncing, for instance — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be announced in the app.
