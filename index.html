Frequently asked

What does TwoUp do?

It puts two panes on one iPhone screen — a web page, a PDF and a plain-text note in any combination. A pair of panes is saved as a workspace and reopens in one tap, including from the Home Screen through Shortcuts. On a foldable the layout follows the actual screen geometry: it swaps between rows and columns without tearing the panes down, so scroll position and note text survive an unfold.

What does TwoUp Pro unlock?

One thing: keeping more than one saved workspace. Everything else — all three pane types, resizing the divider, launching from Shortcuts — works without paying. Pro is sold two ways: a yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase that never renews.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, pick TwoUp and tap Cancel Subscription. Apple bills the next period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current one ends — a free trial included. The one-time purchase has nothing to cancel: it does not repeat.

I paid on another device — how do I get Pro back?

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple Account that made the purchase, it comes back at no charge. Nothing is tied to an email address, because TwoUp has no accounts.

Where are my workspaces, notes and documents kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. TwoUp has no server and no sign-in, so nothing you write or open is uploaded anywhere. The flip side is worth knowing: your data is not synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Why doesn't an imported PDF appear on my other iPhone?

Importing copies the file into TwoUp on that device only, and those copies are deliberately kept out of your iCloud backup — a 200 MB copy of a file you already have in Files would bloat every backup you make. Your original stays where it was, and re-importing it is one tap.

A site looks wrong or won't load in the web pane

The web pane uses the same engine as Safari, but some sites behave differently in a narrow pane, and a few refuse to be embedded at all. If a site misbehaves, send us the address — that is the kind of report we can act on.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in and pick the purchase. Write to us as well if something in the app caused it — we would rather fix the reason.
